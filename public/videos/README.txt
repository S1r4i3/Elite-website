Home page hero background video
--------------------------------
Put your video here with these exact names:
  hero.mp4   (required)  - H.264, 1920x1080, 10-20 second loop, under ~6 MB
  hero.webm  (optional)  - smaller WebM version
  hero.jpg   (optional)  - still frame shown while the video loads

Good free sources (free for commercial use, no attribution needed):
  https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/accountant/
  https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/tax%20documents/
  https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/calculator%20finance/
  https://pixabay.com/videos/search/accounting/

Tips: pick calm footage (hands on a calculator, reviewing documents, a laptop
with spreadsheets, a professional consultation). The site darkens the video
automatically so the headline stays readable. Until a file is here, the hero
shows its normal background.
